Child Safety Standards

Atlas by Atlas Ascent LLC · Published: October 6, 2026

Atlas Ascent LLC ("we," "us," "our") publishes these standards to describe how the Atlas app ("App") prevents, detects, and responds to child sexual abuse and exploitation ("CSAE") and child sexual abuse material ("CSAM"). We maintain a zero-tolerance policy toward CSAE and CSAM in any form, on any surface of the App.

1. Prohibited Content and Conduct

The following are strictly prohibited on Atlas and result in immediate enforcement action:

  • CSAM of any kind, including real, altered, or generated imagery sexualizing minors
  • Grooming, sexual advances toward, or sexualization of minors
  • Sextortion, trafficking, or any facilitation of child sexual exploitation
  • Content or behavior that endangers the safety or wellbeing of a minor

These prohibitions apply to all user-generated content on Atlas, including posts, photos, videos, comments, profiles, and messages, and are incorporated into our Terms of Service.

2. Minimum Age

Atlas is intended for users 16 years of age and older. We do not knowingly permit accounts for children under 16, and we remove accounts identified as belonging to underage users (see our Privacy Policy).

3. Reporting Child Safety Concerns

  • In-app: users can report any post, comment, or account from directly within the App, including a dedicated child safety report category.
  • By email: anyone, including people without an Atlas account, can report concerns to support@atlasfit.app with the subject line "Child Safety Report."

Child safety reports are prioritized ahead of all other moderation work and reviewed promptly by a human.

4. Enforcement

When we identify CSAE or CSAM, we take immediate action, including:

  • Removing the content and permanently terminating the responsible account
  • Preserving evidence as required by law
  • Reporting to the appropriate authorities (see Section 5)

5. Reporting to Authorities

We report apparent CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) via the CyberTipline, in accordance with United States federal law (18 U.S.C. § 2258A), and to other regional or national authorities where required. We cooperate with law enforcement investigations and respond to valid legal process.

6. Compliance

We comply with applicable child safety laws in the jurisdictions where Atlas operates, and we review these standards and our prevention practices as the App and the law evolve.
