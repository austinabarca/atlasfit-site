Delete Your Atlas Account

Atlas by Atlas Ascent LLC · app.atlasfit.mobile

You can permanently delete your Atlas account and the personal data associated with it at any time, using either of the methods below. This page applies to the Atlas app on both iOS and Android.

⚠️ Deletion is permanent

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, workout history, personal records, progress photos, social posts, and all other personal data. This cannot be undone, and we cannot recover your data afterward.

Option 1: Delete from inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open Atlas and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Settings (from your profile).
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm, then confirm the final prompt.

Your account and data are deleted immediately and you will be signed out.

Option 2: Request deletion by email (no app required)

If you have already uninstalled the app or can no longer sign in, email us at support@atlasfit.app with the subject line "Account Deletion Request", sent from the email address associated with your Atlas account (or include that address in your message so we can verify ownership).

We will verify the request and complete the deletion within 30 days, confirming by reply once it is done.

What is deleted, and what may be retained

  • Deleted: your profile and account information, workout history and personal records, body metrics, progress photos, social posts and comments, and all other personal data, permanently removed within 30 days.
  • May be retained: limited data where required for legal compliance, fraud prevention, or legitimate business purposes, and aggregated, anonymized data that cannot identify you (used for analytics).

For full details, see our Privacy Policy (Data Retention section).

Questions

If you have any trouble deleting your account, contact support@atlasfit.app and we will help.
